# Portrait asset

The hero uses the locally supplied `assets/portraits/thiruma.png` image, copied to `public/portraits/thiruma.png` so Angular serves it as `/portraits/thiruma.png`. The particle crowd also uses the locally supplied `assets/portraits/Red and Blue Rally Silhouettes.png`, copied to `public/portraits/red-blue-rally.png`. If the portrait asset cannot load, the hero shows a named typographic fallback rather than substituting another person's face.
